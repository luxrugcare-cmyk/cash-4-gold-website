# Cash 4 Gold — Local Directory & Citation Plan

Adapted from the directory-submissions framework. SaaS tiers (Product Hunt, G2, AI
directories) do not apply — this is a local Johannesburg gold buyer / pawnbroker, so
the relevant layers are: map listings, South African business directories, classifieds,
vertical/trust listings, and reviews.

---

## 1. Readiness assessment

### Hard blocks — resolve BEFORE submitting anything

| # | Blocker | Why it blocks | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Domain not consolidated** — `cash4gold.co.za` (old) vs `cash-4-gold.co.za` (new) | Every citation permanently points at one URL. Splitting NAP signals across two domains dilutes local ranking. | Decide canonical = `cash-4-gold.co.za`; 301-redirect the old domain before Week 1. |

| 3 | **SAPS dealer registration number is placeholder** | Directories and GBP list "registered dealer" claims; unverified claims risk Consumer Protection Act issues and competitor reports. | Get the real number, then add to About page + GBP profile + major listings. |
| 4 | **Postal code unconfirmed** | GBP and most directories require a full postal address. | Confirm Delarey postal code with client; use identical value everywhere. |

## 2. Canonical NAP (use EXACTLY this string everywhere)

soften local prominence. Copy-paste, never retype:

```
Business name:  Cash 4 Gold
Address:        20 6th Street, Delarey, Johannesburg, [POSTAL CODE]
Phone:          010 023 6649  (primary listing number)
WhatsApp:       072 792 9452  (secondary field where offered)
Email:          info@cash-4-gold.co.za
Website:        https://cash-4-gold.co.za/
Hours:          Mon–Fri 09:00–22:00
```

Note: the Facebook page is named "Cash4Gold South Africa" — one word, plus a suffix.
Either rename it to "Cash 4 Gold" or accept the variant; do not introduce a third form.

## 5. Reviews protocol (adapted 10-in-30)

- Reviews are the biggest conversion lever after GBP itself. Zero-review listings die.
- Channel: **WhatsApp** — the client already holds 1:1 customer relationships there.
- Script: after a completed payout, send: *"Thanks for using Cash 4 Gold! Would you
  mind leaving a quick Google review? It takes 30 seconds: [direct review link]"* —
  direct link only, never a form.
- Target: 20 past customers asked → 10 Google reviews in 30 days.
- Ask once; follow up once after 5 days. Never incentivize Google reviews (against
  Google policy) — a thank-you is fine, payment is not.
- Second surface: Hellopeter (claim profile first) and the existing Facebook page.
- The site's review-CTA section already links to Google/Facebook — update the links
  once GBP is verified.
