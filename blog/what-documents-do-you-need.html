What documents do you need to pawn or sell?

There's nothing worse than a wasted trip. Here's the complete checklist of what to bring — whether you're selling a chain, pawning a watch or borrowing against a car.

South African law requires pawnbrokers and second-hand goods dealers to record who sells or pawns every item — it's what keeps stolen goods traceable and honest customers safe. That means paperwork, but far less than most people fear. Here's the full list.

Everyone brings: ID and proof of address

You must be 18 or older, and we must record who you are. Bring:

  • ✓ Green barcoded ID book or smart card — original, not a photocopy
  • ✓ Passport with a valid permit if you're not a South African citizen
  • ✓ Proof of address — a utility bill, bank statement or lease under 3 months old

If your documents don't show your current address, bring the utility bill for it anyway — the ID proves who you are, the bill proves where you live.

Gold, silver and coins

Nothing extra required. Broken chains, rings, scrap and coins need no certificates. If you have original certificates or boxes for coins and bullion, bring them — they can improve your offer.

Luxury watches

Watches sell and pawn fine without papers, but the original box and papers (the warranty card with the serial number) typically add real value to the offer. Bring them if you have them.

Vehicles

  • ✓ Registration certificate (NaTIS / RC1) in your name
  • ✓ Your driver's licence
  • ✓ Settlement letter if the vehicle is still under finance

The car must be registered in your name. If it isn't (an inherited or gifted vehicle), WhatsApp us first — there are ways to handle it, but they need sorting out before the deal.

Boats and jet skis

Bring whatever proof of ownership you have: the registration papers, bill of sale, or trailer licence papers. Jet skis and trailers are registered with eNaTIS — those papers speed everything up.

What we do with your information

Our SAPS dealer registration requires us to keep a record of every transaction — your details, the item, and its identifying marks. Your information is stored securely, never shared with marketers, and handled under POPIA. See our privacy policy for the details.

That's it — one ID, one proof of address, plus the papers for your specific item. If you're unsure about your situation, WhatsApp us before you drive over and we'll confirm in two minutes.
