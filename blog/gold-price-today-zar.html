The gold price today — and why it never stops moving.

Two numbers decide what your gold is worth in rands today: the global spot price and the dollar-rand exchange rate. Here's how they work together, and how to track them yourself.

Search "gold price today" and you'll find dollar figures per ounce that seem to have nothing to do with what a Johannesburg buyer offers for a 9ct chain. Here's the bridge between the two — it's shorter than you'd think.

Number one: the spot price

Gold trades around the world 24 hours a day. Its current market value — the spot price — is quoted in US dollars per troy ounce (31.1 grams). Wars, inflation data, central bank decisions and ordinary trading all push it up and down daily.

Number two: the dollar-rand rate

You're paid in rands, so the dollar price has to be converted — and the rand moves against the dollar every day too. A day when gold is flat in dollars can still be a better day for your payout if the rand weakened. This is why the rand price of gold is the one that matters to you, and why it changes even when the dollar price doesn't.
