Gold & Money · 29 Sep 2026

We buy more than gold: silver, diamonds and bullion.

Grandmother's silver cutlery, a diamond ring from a previous chapter, gold bars from a smart decision years ago — all of it can be turned into cash the same day.

The name on the door says Cash 4 Gold, but gold is only part of what crosses our counter. Here's how we value the other precious metals and stones — and what to know before you bring yours in.

Sterling silver

Silver jewellery, coins and cutlery marked 925 (sterling) are valued by weight against the silver price — the same structure as gold, at silver's different price point. Heavily plated items (EPNS, silverplate cutlery) have little metal value, and we'll tell you that honestly rather than waste your time. Old Rand and Mandela silver coins are worth asking about individually.

Diamonds

Loose or set, certified or not — we buy diamonds. Value comes down to the classic four Cs: carat, cut, colour, clarity. Certificates (GIA and similar) speed things up and support the price, but their absence isn't a dealbreaker — stones are assessed on the counter. Even small side-stones in gold rings add to a payout. If you're simply after the best outcome for an inherited piece, start with our free valuation.

Bullion bars and coins

Investment gold — bars, Krugerrands, Maple Leafs, Eagles — trades against the day's spot price with a small margin, exactly like selling Krugerrands. Original packaging and certificates help but aren't required. Weights from 1 gram upward are all worth a conversation.

The process is identical to gold

Everything is tested and weighed in front of you, the pricing basis is explained, and you're paid the same day. Sell or pawn — whichever suits you; see pawning vs selling if you're weighing the two. Bring your ID and proof of address and that's it.
