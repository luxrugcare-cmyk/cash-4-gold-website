Privacy Policy.

Cash 4 Gold respects your privacy and processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

Cash 4 Gold ("we", "us") is a buyer and pawnbroker of gold, jewellery, luxury watches, vehicles, boats and other valuable goods, operating from 20 6th Street, Delarey, Johannesburg. We are the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy. Contact: info@cash-4-gold.co.za or 010 023 6649.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the transaction, we may collect: your name and contact details (phone number, email address); identity information required by the Second-Hand Goods Act, 2009 for second-hand and pawn transactions; details of the items you sell or pawn, including photographs and descriptions you send us; vehicle and boat registration details where applicable; and records of transactions you enter into with us.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to: value and process your transactions; comply with our legal obligations, including record-keeping under the Second-Hand Goods Act and SAPS dealer requirements; pay you and keep records of payments; communicate with you about your enquiry, collection or loan; and protect the security of our premises and stored goods (CCTV where applicable).

4. Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We disclose it only where legally required (for example to SAPS under the Second-Hand Goods Act), to payment providers processing your EFT, or to professional advisers bound by confidentiality.

5. Security & retention

We store records securely and retain transaction records only as long as the law requires us to. Physical documents are kept in locked storage and electronic records are access-controlled. You can ask us at any time what information of yours we hold.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you (subject to legal record-keeping duties), to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa. To exercise any of these rights, email info@cash-4-gold.co.za.

7. Website

Our enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message you control before sending. We do not run advertising trackers on this site, and we do not collect analytics that identify you personally.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page. Last updated: September 2026.
